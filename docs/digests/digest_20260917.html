🚀 Opportunity of the Day2 min read

The single best thing to build right now.

Agentic-Policy-Compliance-Engine (APCE)

  • The gap: As highlighted in the Compositional Policy Violations paper, existing governance is step-scoped (input/output rails), leaving multi-step agentic workflows vulnerable to "policy drift" where individual safe steps aggregate into a prohibited outcome.
  • Why now: The convergence of OpenAI’s new misalignment framework and the Independence-Graded Audit Protocol creates a market demand for automated, cross-turn compliance verification that doesn't exist in current LLM-as-a-judge setups.
  • Build as: A middleware library (OSS) that sits between the orchestrator and the model, maintaining a "policy-state-graph" to validate the entire trajectory against high-level constraints.
  • Wedge & moat: Start with regulated industries (FinTech/HCLS) where "step-level" compliance is insufficient for audit; the moat is the proprietary library of "compositional policy templates" that evolve with regulatory changes.
  • Already heating up: (speculative — no direct product validation yet, but strong research momentum in Compositional Policy Violations and Audit Protocols).
  • Closest existing solution: Guardrails AI; it focuses on input/output validation but lacks the "trajectory-state-graph" required to detect multi-step compositional violations.
  • First step this week: Prototype a "Policy-Graph-Validator" that takes a JSON-based workflow trace and checks it against a set of state-dependent rules, measuring the false-positive rate against a standard ReAct agent loop.
