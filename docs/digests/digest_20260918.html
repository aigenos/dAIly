🚀 Opportunity of the Day2 min read

The single best thing to build right now.

Agentic-Trust-Provenance-Gateway (ATPG)

  • The gap: Current agentic platforms lack a verifiable "chain of custody" for agent actions, leaving them vulnerable to the fingerprinting and sandbox escapes identified in recent research.
  • Why now: The convergence of the Internet of Agents trust discovery research and the immediate need for MCP-level authorization makes a unified provenance layer both technically feasible and commercially urgent.
  • Build as: A middleware proxy (sidecar) that intercepts agent-to-agent (A2A) calls, cryptographically signing the trajectory and verifying the "intent-to-tool" mapping before execution.
  • Wedge & moat: Start by securing enterprise coding agents (e.g., ZCode, Cursor, Windsurf) against unauthorized data exfiltration; compound by becoming the standard "trust-layer" for multi-agent orchestration platforms.
  • Already heating up: High interest in OpenSpec and the Skillsync launch suggest developers are actively looking for ways to standardize and secure agentic workflows.
  • Closest existing solution: AWS Bedrock AgentCore Gateway; however, it is cloud-locked and lacks cross-platform trust discovery for the broader "Internet of Agents."
  • First step this week: Prototype a "Provenance Header" middleware that injects a signed JWT into every MCP tool call, verifying the agent's identity and the specific task context.
